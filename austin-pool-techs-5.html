Quick pool help

Why is my pool cloudy?

Usually circulation, filtration or water chemistry. Check the filter pressure, the pump and the chemical levels. A clarifier or a filter clean may be needed.

How often should I test the water?

Home pools do best with testing from daily to weekly, depending on how much the pool is used.

When should I backwash the filter?

When the gauge reads about 10 pounds above its clean reading, or at least monthly. If it will not drop, the sand or cartridges may need work.

My pump hums or will not start

Often a failed capacitor or a seized impeller. Pool electrical work is dangerous, so call a professional.

My heater will not turn on

Safety switches stop it when water is not flowing. If circulation is good and it still will not fire, call us.
